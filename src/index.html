Emulsion

A darkroom in one file. Raw development, film stocks, halation and grain, running entirely on this computer.

Or drop photos or a folder anywhere on this window. Raw (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, DNG, ORF, RW2 and more), JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF are supported. Your originals are never changed: edits are stored in a catalog file beside them.

This browser cannot keep a folder open or write a catalog file, so edits are stored in the browser and exports go to your downloads. Chrome or Edge gives you the full library.
